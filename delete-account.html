Delete your Habits Monitor account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time. Deletion removes your account, all habits, completion history, and reminders from our servers. This cannot be undone.

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Habits Monitor and sign in.
  2. On the Today screen, tap the ⚙ Settings icon (top right).
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone → tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and all server data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — By email

If you can no longer access the app, email cojocaru.serghei@gmail.com from the address associated with your account with the subject "Account deletion request". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email / linked Google or Facebook identity)
  • All habits, completion records, and reminders stored on our servers

Crash diagnostics already collected are anonymized and cannot be linked back to your account. The local copy of your data is removed when you uninstall the app.

See also our Privacy Policy.
